## The Icon Upgrade Story

### What Icons Were Wrong?
These 6 icons didn't exist in react-icons/fa Font Awesome 5:
- FaFileLines (Font Awesome 6+)
- FaCircleInfo (Font Awesome 6+)
- FaRightFromBracket (Variant doesn't exist)
- FaRightToBracket (Variant doesn't exist)
- FaZap (Wrong name)
- FaSparkles (Font Awesome 6+)

### What We Replaced Them With?
Smart, semantic alternatives that work perfectly:

```
Blog Posts       ← FaFileLines becomes FaBook 📖
Information      ← FaCircleInfo becomes FaInfoCircle ℹ️
Sign Out         ← FaRightFromBracket becomes FaSignOutAlt 🚪
Sign In          ← FaRightToBracket becomes FaSignInAlt 🚪
Speed/Power      ← FaZap becomes FaBolt ⚡
Featured/Star    ← FaSparkles becomes FaStar ⭐
```
